ABOUT

Spider-Man, created by writer Stan Lee and writer-artist Steve Ditka, is one of the most iconic and enduring superheroes in popular culture. He first appeared in "Amazing Fantasy" #15, published by Marvel Comics in 1962. Spider-Man's origin story tells of Peter Parker, a high school student whose life changed dramatically after being bitten by a radioactive spider.
